5. METHODOLOGY EXPLANATION
================================================================================

VIF (Variance Inflation Factor) Calculation:
  For each feature X_i, VIF_i measures how much the variance of the
  estimated coefficient is inflated by collinearity with other features.

  Formula: VIF_i = 1 / (1 - R²_i)
  Where R²_i is from regressing X_i on all other features.

Interpretation:
  • VIF = 1: No correlation with other features (ideal)
  • VIF < 5: Low multicollinearity (acceptable)
  • 5 ≤ VIF < 10: Moderate multicollinearity (monitor)
  • VIF ≥ 10: High multicollinearity (action required)
