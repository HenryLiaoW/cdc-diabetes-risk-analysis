5. Critical Evaluation: Why Cluster in Original 5-Feature Space?
================================================================================

Decision: Perform clustering in ORIGINAL 5-feature space, NOT PCA space

Rationale:
  ✓ Clinical Interpretability:
    - Original features (chronic_disease_burden, ses_vulnerability, etc.)
      directly map to INTERVENTION STRATEGIES
    - PCA components are linear combinations lacking clinical meaning
    - Policy-makers need actionable dimensions, not abstract 'PC1'

  ✓ Feature Engineering Already Reduced Dimensionality:
    - Started with 21 original BRFSS variables
    - Aggregated into 5 composite features (76% reduction)
    - Further PCA reduction would sacrifice hard-won interpretability

  ✓ Low Multicollinearity Confirmed:
    - VIF analysis (Figure 12) showed max VIF = 1.12 (well below threshold)
    - PCA variance distribution is relatively balanced (no single dominant PC)
    - This indicates features are already sufficiently independent

  ✓ Computational Feasibility:
    - 5 dimensions is manageable for K-Means (N=229,781 samples)
    - PCA reduction typically justified for D>20 or computational constraints
    - No such constraints exist in our case

  ✗ PCA Trade-offs:
    - Dimensionality reduction ALWAYS loses information
    - Even 3 PCs only retain 69.3% variance
    - Lost variance may contain clinically important distinctions
